# A cinematic look in a locked projection view

Use for a tabletop look-frame or lighting repair, not as a universal film style.
Receive the actual camera/mapping/plate anchors and performance focal action. Do
not tilt the camera, introduce shallow focus or stretch the raster for drama.

## Define before rendering

Record `frame/version | fixed geometry | first read | second read | quiet region |
motivated light sources | palette/material separation | delivery-scale rejects`.

- First read is the story action, not necessarily the brightest or largest plate.
- Shape the character and contact with a localized key; reduce competing fill or
  background contrast. A lantern should affect nearby surfaces, not just glow as
  a flat icon. Shadow direction must obey the seat/mapping contract.
- Separate satin cloth, diffuse skin, glazed ceramic, aged metal and wet food by
  highlight character, not by assigning the same gloss to every object.
- Use a restrained warm practical/cool ambient relationship when it fits the
  reference. Do not bake that palette into every future scene.
- Preserve visible feet/hands/receiver in shadow. Crushed blacks, blanket bloom,
  exposure pumping and depth-of-field blur are not shortcuts to cinema.
- Reduce high-frequency ornament near a tiny actor. Frame the action with set
  structure and negative space; density alone does not make a palace.

## Two early checks

1. Inspect a silhouette/contact view for physical readability, separate from the
   finished materials. A clean debug view is not a style target.
2. Render a representative style frame in the **same registered view** with the
   intended materials/light. Compare at full size and at actual seat/tile scale.
   For four-seat content, inspect each rotated tile and the shared center; the
   correct solution need not be a single global cinematic focal point.

Save a brief decision: accepted internally for a short test / failed / unverified,
with failed variables. A good still does not approve acting or contact. Do not
carry old checks to changed geometry or pretend a wider diagnostic is the master.

## Hand off the real result

Pass the actual rendered look frame and matching scene/camera/version, not only
adjectives or an oblique mood image. If an oblique reference is also useful, label
it material/light only and explicitly exclude its lens, inventory and layout.
For a generative call, retain high-fidelity anchors/contact while allowing only
the declared appearance/performance freedoms. Current budget still governs calls.

Production synthesis informed by official Disney
[visual development](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/visual-development/) and
[lighting](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/lighting/) process descriptions,
checked 2026-09-30. These support story-led composition/color and light-guided
attention, not a claim of studio-level quality or a numerical lighting recipe.
