# Storyboard ↔ simple-mesh previs

Use this reference only when the user requests mesh previs or the selected work
needs a 3D check of a specific unresolved path, occlusion, contact or timing issue.
The current tabletop workflow starts with Nano Banana plate/character images and
Kling scene generation; completing a storyboard does not trigger Blender work.
Keep the chosen scope. A local problem does not require a whole-film previs.

1. Reuse beat sheet, FPS, selected interval, camera, aspect and anchors. Use primitive
   actors, open vessels, food blocks and graphic masks. No detailed skinning,
   texture polish, simulation or paid generation unless separately required.
2. Within that scope show every meaningful event: tool source, handling, travel, landing, coverage,
   edit, reveal, settle and ending asset position. Mark proxy-only anatomy.
   An explicitly requested whole-duration previs must cover the whole story.
3. A full-screen wipe must cover every corner at the hidden cut. Inspect the
   actual previs frames; four plate masks are not equivalent.
4. Save editable scene, encoded video and shared beat/time mapping. Supplied end
   assets may be composited unchanged; record this exception to simple meshes.
5. If boards are part of the requested comparison, put them beside the video with
   shared time/seek controls. Small screens
   may stack. Do not show an old previs beside changed boards as a matching version.
6. Verify duration/frames and inspect critical boundaries. Primitive paths do not
   qualify a human rig or prove physics/acting. List proxy limitations explicitly.
7. Respect any requested creative confirmation and existing task authority. Do not
   add a previs confirmation gate to image-only work. Previs is not paid authority.
