# Research basis: motivated animation and commercial transitions

Reviewed 2026-09-30. Primary studio, tool-maker and author sources below.
These are craft references, not controlled evidence that a prompt improves a
video model. No proprietary artwork or private project media is copied.

## What the sources support

1. **Disney Animation — [Story](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/story/)**
   integrates narrative, emotion, timing, staging and framing in storyboarding,
   refining rough drawings for clarity. Our application: evaluate readable
   relationships before expensive final images, not only a written script.
2. **Disney Animation — [Layout](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/layout/)**
   describes rough animation, props, cameras and temporary effects used to test
   staging and edits. Our application: rough blocking can test an entry, handoff
   or wipe before detailed modeling.
3. **Disney Animation — [Animation](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/animation/)**
   identifies staging, anticipation, timing and follow-through alongside anatomy
   and weight. Our application: show preparation and consequence, without forcing
   a pause between every phase of a performance.
4. **BUCK — [Illumina](https://buck.co/work/illumina)**
   explains its brand-film process through meaning, connections between shots,
   mixed media and logical or magical transitions. This is the studio's account,
   not an effectiveness test. Our application: commercial fantasy can be coherent
   without pretending every graphic effect is literal physics.
5. **Adobe — [Match cuts](https://www.adobe.com/in/creativecloud/video/discover/match-cut.html)**
   distinguishes visual, action and audio connections and recommends preplanning
   composition, movement and sound. Our application: specify the shared feature
   and what changes; a generic dissolve is not a causal repair.
6. **Chen et al., 2022 — [Match Cutting: Finding Cuts with Smooth Visual Transitions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05766)**
   studies candidate shot pairs through framing, composition and action similarities.
   Only the abstract and project summary informed this skill. This does not establish
   story motivation, physical plausibility or a model prompting recipe.

## Production inferences, not quotations or universal laws

- An entity ledger prevents “hidden” from silently becoming “nonexistent.”
- Separate literal action, fantasy and editorial change: a wipe is not simulated
  cooking or collision resolution.
- Before/coverage/reveal/after boards expose ambiguous instructions. Panel count
  depends on the actual uncertainty; four is not a universal requirement.
- Full-frame coverage is geometrical: an uncovered corner contradicts it. Inspect
  alpha/coverage during implementation, not merely the prompt.
- A valid cause can still be unreadable. Actual playback at delivery scale is
  needed; still boards do not establish audience comprehension.
- Motifs and sound can connect acts, but cannot excuse broken grips or guarantee
  continuity across generated clips.

Do not claim these commercials were reconstructed frame by frame or their
audience response measured here. Use the ideas, not studio names as prompt decoration.
