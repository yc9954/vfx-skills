## Visual Skills

`skills/visual-image` and `skills/visual-video` derive from Serge Shima's
[smixs/visual-skills](https://github.com/smixs/visual-skills), inspected revision
`92be33a5a73325fb3d8e0c73b22744b114e2a90e`.
Their CC BY 4.0 LICENSE, NOTICE and SOURCE files are preserved in each skill.
Local tabletop/projection and safety adaptations are identified there and in the Git history.

## Local skills and code

The other ten VFX skills and the orchestration utilities are consolidated from the user's local workflow
and edits for this project. No new blanket open-source license is granted by this restructuring.
Publishing this source does not relicense third-party materials. Review their applicable
terms before including or redistributing assets outside this source surface.

## Excluded dependencies

The former Kimodo checkout, its license and Git bundle, Blender/Unreal project files and third-party assets
remain in the local legacy archive. They are not vendored into this source repository.
Individual research links are citations, not copied or relicensed research works.
