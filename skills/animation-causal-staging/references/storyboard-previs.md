# Storyboard ↔ simple-mesh previs

Standing preference for this tabletop project: every completed storyboard is
paired with a whole-duration simple-mesh video before final production approval.
Latest user instructions override it. Do not impose it on unrelated projects,
render-status questions or research-only tasks.

1. Reuse beat sheet, FPS, whole duration, camera, aspect and anchors. Use primitive
   actors, open vessels, food blocks and graphic masks. No detailed skinning,
   texture polish, simulation or paid generation unless separately required.
2. Show every meaningful event: tool source, handling, travel, landing, coverage,
   edit, reveal, settle and ending asset position. Mark proxy-only anatomy.
3. A full-screen wipe must cover every corner at the hidden cut. Inspect the
   actual previs frames; four plate masks are not equivalent.
4. Save editable scene, encoded video and shared beat/time mapping. Supplied end
   assets may be composited unchanged; record this exception to simple meshes.
5. Put boards and video side by side with shared time/seek controls. Small screens
   may stack. Do not show an old previs beside changed boards as a matching version.
6. Verify duration/frames and inspect critical boundaries. Primitive paths do not
   qualify a human rig or prove physics/acting. List proxy limitations explicitly.
7. Respect final confirmation. This low-cost previs is not authority for a paid job.
