# Animation causality review

Review as a skeptical viewer, not as the author defending a solver. Could the
depicted cause produce the depicted result, and can a viewer read that cause?
Continuous transforms, a physics-engine label and green metrics are not enough.

## Enter at the review stage

- Review the actual candidate before its approval report. Identify source cuts
  and candidate/scene versions; filenames and a prior green report are not proof.
- If there is no candidate and the request is only to study a reference, do
  reference analysis instead. Do not invent missing production artifacts.
- Review requests produce findings. Repair or render only when authorized by the
  current request. Do not launch a whole-film render to investigate a complaint.

## Whole-scene scope, conditional detail

Inventory the whole story once: actors/body parts, props/ingredients, supports,
containers, rigging, liquid/effects, camera and sound. Trace each meaningful
entity's identity, geometry, initial/final location and support or attachment.
For each action record preparation, contact, effort, consequence and recovery.
Do not narrow the inventory to the user's named defect or demand every possible
geometric pair. Then read all references relevant to that inventory:

| When present | Required detail |
|---|---|
| Disputed object appearances, effect motivation or covered scene edits | Compare the existing [causal staging](../animation-causal-staging/SKILL.md) beat contract if supplied: source, cue, path, result, persistence and full/local coverage. Missing planning does not excuse visible faults. |
| Character travel, balance or hands | [Locomotion and acting](references/locomotion-and-acting.md) for physical/body evidence |
| Character acting, thought/emotional beats, gaze/face, timing, appeal or moving holds | Read the shared [character performance QA](../character-performance-animation/references/performance-qa.md) and applicable performance reference; keep candidate verdict ownership here |
| Loaded reach/lift/carry/pour, disputed ROM, weight transfer or kinetic chain | Read the shared [motion contract](../human-motion-biomechanics/references/motion-language.md) and affected [joint](../human-motion-biomechanics/references/joint-rom-and-coordination.md), [load](../human-motion-biomechanics/references/load-balance-and-handling.md) or [grasp](../human-motion-biomechanics/references/grasp-and-tool-use.md) reference; keep verdict ownership here |
| Rig qualification, joint deformation or disputed whole-body coordination | [Full-body rig review](references/full-body-rig-review.md), for the affected actions/regions |
| Dance, musical performance, repeated motions, ensembles or clip transitions | [Motion and dance review](references/motion-and-dance-review.md) |
| Articulated fingers, grasping, wrist twists or disputed hand quality | [Hand/grasp review](references/hand-grasp-review.md) in addition to locomotion and tools |
| Handling, containers, rigging, liquids or contact effects | [Tools and effects](references/tools-and-effects.md) |
| Food/fluid identity, viscosity, grains, wetness or steam is a disputed quality | Use installed `food-vfx-director` for specialized material gates; retain whole-scene verdict here |
| Tests/approval claims, changed candidates, or delivery decisions | [Evidence and release](references/evidence-and-release.md) |
| Saved-scene collision reports, excluded hand/cloth geometry, support gaps or disputed grouping | [Evaluated contact evidence](references/evaluated-contact-evidence.md) |

A reference split reduces irrelevant reading, not inspection coverage. Mark
unobserved or omitted relationships as unverified, never passed.
Specialist food findings map back to causal/contact and presentation categories;
they do not approve the rest of the animation or technical delivery.

## Core evidence rules

- Keep a compact ledger: entity pair + interval, intended cause, actual controller,
  candidate version, observation, independent measurement, exclusions and verdict.
- For human handling, bind the candidate to the same versioned contract used for blocking/prompting:
  load assumption, start/end supports, grip roles, working-range status, kinetic chain,
  weight transfer, phase order and settle. A missing contract does not excuse visible failure;
  it leaves unsupported intent claims unverified.
- For character acting, bind the candidate to the same performance-contract version:
  objective, thought/emotional beats, key poses, timing accents, gaze/face, gesture
  hierarchy, partner reactions, moving holds and lead/follow/settle order. A physical
  pass cannot fill a missing performance claim, and a prompt cannot verify playback.
- Inspect critical interactions before first possible touch, through contact and
  release, and afterward. A legal pickup cannot erase an illegal approach.
- Distinguish literal action, declared fantasy and editorial replacement. A wipe
  can conceal a scene edit but cannot prove an unseen safe pickup. For requested
  full-screen coverage inspect every corner during the cut; local masks are not
  equivalent. Match cuts and sound bridges can motivate edits, not establish physics.
- Measure evaluated scene geometry, not a target compared to the function that
  generated it. Name units, tolerances, surfaces, phases and sampling resolution.
- Root clearance, ankle drift, wrist reach, static overlap, containment and swept
  collision are different claims. Sparse no-hit frames do not prove continuous safety.
- A 2D foreground tracker is not joint-level 3D reconstruction. Hidden contacts
  remain uncertain. Do not invent a mechanism or species to fill missing evidence.
- Normal-speed performance, slow contact inspection and still-image review are
  separate observations. If playback or audition is unavailable, explicitly retain
  unverified acting/audio; never claim to have watched or heard it.
- A treatment, reference-role map, prompt check or intended state transition is
  design evidence, not evidence that the generated candidate obeyed it. Review
  the actual result against both its contact contract and reference-grounded
  style/reject criteria. Good aesthetics cannot excuse a broken cause, and zero
  measured penetration cannot establish readable purpose or appealing acting.

## Verdict and next step

Use **verified for stated scope**, **failed**, or **unverified**. Separate causal/
contact safety from character performance, then materials/presentation, audio and
technical delivery. Within performance, keep objective/read, thought→action, key
poses, timing/rhythm, gaze/face, hierarchy/overlap, personality/appeal, partner/prop
acting and end state as independent claims. Do not average physical and performance
scores. A failed user-required category blocks quality approval; an absent test is
not success. A user challenge reopens the affected claim and adjacent phases,
rather than becoming a “stylized limitation” defended with old metrics.

Report timestamped findings and a bounded next step. Repair causality before
timing/polish when implementation is authorized; preserve the intended action and
duration. Do not hide contradictions with framing or silently invent supports.
Keep technical file success even when quality approval is withdrawn. An available
trial may be delivered as a trial, not relabelled as an approved finished film.

For a multi-stage job with an existing project record, follow the installed
[handoff contract](../miniature-film-director/references/handoff-contract.md).
If that companion is unavailable, record candidate identity, scoped verdicts,
evidence and next action in the existing project notes. Do not create production
state files merely for a review-only question. This skill grants no new permission
for paid calls, uploads, new tasks or deletion.
