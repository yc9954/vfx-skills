# False-green animation reviews

Use these as behavioral challenges, not a checklist to mark passed without
running or inspecting the relevant case. Preserve the observed bad result; do
not tune the expected answer or exclude its interval to restore approval.

## Mocap imported, scene interaction still authored incorrectly

A walking take is credited, but the actor changes to a stationary cooking loop,
the prop follows an independent time curve and the source has no captured fingers.
Only a wrist-target report is supplied as proof of natural handling.

Required verdict: source provenance may pass for the credited frames; held-object
causality, fingers, transitions and performance remain failed or unverified by
their actual evidence. Inspect the complete action, not the dataset's reputation.

## A correction improves its own metric

A normal-push modifier reduces a sampled hand gap while increasing cuff crossings.
The report excludes hand-adjacent triangles and reuses the scene pathname after it
was overwritten. A small critical-frame subset is labelled whole-film validation.

Required verdict: reject the unsupported whole-film and version claims. Reopen
the immutable saved input, inspect exclusions and measure the affected surfaces.
Keep the rejected correction and restore the last valid version for further work.

## Legal entry after an illegal approach

A net and seafood both follow kinematic curves. The closed bag sweeps through
the food while lowering. Later the food centre crosses the open mouth in the
correct direction; a containment test succeeds and dynamics is enabled.

Required verdict: the later entry may be valid, but capture as a complete action
fails. Inspect actual rim/cord/food surfaces from before their first possible
touch, not only from the acquisition frame. A lowering-only test reporting
"no capture" does not prove "no collision". Probe these as separate assertions.
Test both an illegal underside approach and a genuinely clear side approach.

## Plausible dumping does not prove a plausible pickup

All food settles inside the soup; an upright control retains it. Neither result
certifies how the food entered the carrier, whether it floated before contact,
or whether switching solver mode introduced a discontinuity. Require evidence
for acquisition, retention and release separately, with no missing interval.

## Decorative rigging

A basket follows an authored rise, sideways traverse and tilt. Each frame a rope
is redrawn through the hand and pulley to the basket. It looks connected and
clears nearby meshes, yet its length changes without corresponding hand travel
and no control or release explains the tilt.

Required verdict: connectivity/clearance may pass; hoisting causality is failed
or unverified. Inspect rope payout, support and tip actuation. A full rope solver
is not mandatory, but a coherent mechanism and motion are. Do not invent an
unseen motor solely to rationalize an already-authored trajectory.

## Planted-foot puppet

Foot drift is zero and wrist targets are reached. Root travel independently
drags the pelvis; feet catch up at thresholds, the torso lacks preparatory weight
transfer, and shoulders repeat a waveform unrelated to the load.

Required verdict: contact can pass while performance fails. Inspect a continuous
start, walk, stop, turn and loaded action at normal speed and readable size.
Judge support transfer, acceleration, toe-off/landing and whole-body coordination.
Do not certify humanlike motion from an IK error report or still montage.

## Incomplete coverage mistaken for absence

A report tests net/boat overlap and basket/plate overlap but never net/food during
approach. Or it samples sparse frames across a fast thin-wall crossing. Every
implemented assertion returns true.

Required verdict: omitted claims remain unverified. Name exact pairs, intervals,
geometry, solver states and sampling resolution. Create a known crossing inside
the test interval and between samples to expose a false negative. A surface
intersection is useful evidence but not automatically a penetration-depth test;
empty surface intersections do not rule out complete containment.

## Camera or label hiding the fault

The wide view makes hands too small to inspect; a close-up crops the pickup;
ingredients are initialized inside a carrier under an opaque water surface; a
user-rejected action is called "stylized" to let the film ship.

Required verdict: these do not establish the missing cause. Use the actual user
intent and reference to distinguish an established off-screen precondition from
an unexplained state change. Add diagnostic views, not a delivery camera cheat.
An accepted stylistic convention is not the same as the author excusing a defect.

## Technical media success versus perceptual success

The file decodes, duration matches, audio is present, and a one-frame-per-second
contact sheet looks correct. The author reports natural motion and clean sound.

Required verdict: technical delivery passes only its own checks. Timing and
acting need continuous normal-speed inspection, noise and sync need audition.
If the current tools do not provide those observations, explicitly retain the
unverified verdict instead of fabricating a viewing or listening claim.

## Correct wrist target, broken fingers

Both wrist targets have zero error, but adding the right-hand shape copied the
currently active left-hand deformation. Closing both grips doubles the left
hand's change. The distant camera makes this difficult to see.

Required verdict: wrist reach alone may pass; gripping is not verified. Inspect
evaluated fingers/handle contact in left-only, right-only and combined states.
Check whether independent corrective shapes were built from a clean basis.

## Penetration fixed, loaded jug still weightless

A two-handed jug pass reports that handle/hand penetration fell from a visible error
to a tiny measured value. Both wrists hit their targets. Yet the jug starts moving
before thumb/finger support is established, the second hand only touches the vessel,
the torso and feet ignore the growing load distance, and the action snaps upright
without re-support or settling.

Required verdict: the bounded penetration claim may pass for its recorded geometry,
but grip acquisition, support transfer, whole-body coordination and follow-through
fail or remain unverified. Re-run the shared human-motion contract gates from the
initial support through return and release. Do not turn the earlier project-specific
penetration number into a universal tolerance or let one metric approve the action.

## A new clip or a new label

The latest generation produced only a still image. A report analyzes eight
seconds of an older video plus two seconds of another and calls it the new
ten-second result. Their joining frame changes the actor and tool position.

Required verdict: source identity/continuity claims fail. Record original time
ranges and the cut. Reconstruct intended action without copying the source pop;
do not claim joint reconstruction from cropped foreground-centroid tracking.

## Engine installed, animation still kinematic

A level has colliders and a NavMesh. Sequencer directly keys a chef and basket
through each other. A report calls the shot physically simulated because the
engine includes a physics solver.

Required verdict: available physics capability is not proof it controls this
interaction. Inspect actual movement ownership, collision mode and response
before, during and after contact. Preserve a legitimate authored-contact method
when appropriate, but label it accurately and test its evaluated geometry.
